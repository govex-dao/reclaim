How redemption works

Each GOVEX token receives the same proportional share: USDC in the pool × GOVEX redeemed ÷ current GOVEX supply. Estimates update every 30 seconds. The final amount is calculated on-chain when your transaction executes, rounded down to the nearest 0.000001 USDC.

You can redeem some or all of the GOVEX in your wallet. There is no Govex service fee for this claim.
